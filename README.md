# Aequilibrion
### A problem generator for analytical chemistry

## Context
Aequilibrion is a Python-based command line tool for Linux that generates randomized problems in order to practice analytical chemistry. It is structured around the analytical chemistry requirements for the Pharmacobiological Chemistry (QFB) program at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) and, as such, is divided into five modules:
* **Simple Equilibria:** Covers the topics related to analytical chemistry 1, including acid-base, redox, complexation and solubility problems.
* **Simultaneous Equilibria:** Covers the topics related to analytical chemistry 2, including problems that contemplate combinations of the simple equilibria from the first module.
* **Experimental Analysis:** Covers the topics in experimental analysis 1 and 2 that relate to metrology and experimental design, such as error propagation and measurement of uncertainty.
* **Instrumental Analysis:** Covers the topics in experimental analysis 2 and instrumental analysis that relate to electrochemical, chromatographic and spectroscopic techniques.
* **Marine Applications:** Related to my own interests, this module applies the principles from the previous four modules to problems relevant to marine biogeochemistry.
